# Privacy Policy

**App:** BrainBro
**Last updated:** 2026-09-15

BrainBro is a colour logic-puzzle game designed to keep your data yours. Your
privacy is respected by design: **BrainBro does not collect, transmit, sell, or
share your personal data with us or any third party.** BrainBro has no accounts,
no analytics, and no advertising, and it works fully offline.

## What data BrainBro uses

Everything BrainBro keeps is stored **on your device**:

- **Your current game** — the board, your progress, and per-player counts.
- **Saved boards** — puzzles you save or import from a shared code.
- **Statistics** — solve times, best/average per difficulty, accuracy, hints
  used, and your daily streak.
- **Settings** — display mode and board style, difficulty, two-player mode and
  player names, teaching mode, language, and similar preferences.

This information is stored locally using Apple's standard on-device storage
(`UserDefaults`). It stays on your device and is included only in your own
device or iCloud **backups**, which are controlled entirely by you and handled
by Apple.

## Sharing a board

BrainBro lets you share a board as a short **code**. When you share, the code is
handed to the standard iOS share sheet and sent wherever **you** choose (for
example Messages or Mail). BrainBro has no servers and never receives a copy.
Importing a code someone sends you simply recreates that puzzle on your device.

## Notifications

If you enable the optional **daily reminder**, BrainBro schedules a local
notification on your device using Apple's notification system. No information is
sent anywhere; you can turn reminders off any time in the app or in **Settings →
Notifications**.

## What BrainBro does NOT do

- No accounts, sign-in, or registration.
- No analytics, usage tracking, advertising, or profiling.
- No third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
- No developer-operated servers — BrainBro does not send your data to us.
- No access to location, microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or health data.

## Your control over your data

- Reset all statistics from **Settings → Show statistics → Reset statistics**.
- Turn the daily reminder on or off at any time.
- Deleting the BrainBro app removes its local data from that device.

## Children

BrainBro does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
