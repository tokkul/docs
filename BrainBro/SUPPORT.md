# BrainBro — Support

BrainBro is a colourful take on the classic logic-grid puzzle you already know —
solve it on your own, or together with someone on the same board. When you get
stuck, its teaching hints don't just give the answer — they show you *why* the
next move works.

## Contact

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I play?
Fill the grid so every colour appears exactly **once in each row, once in each
column, and once in each 3×3 box**. That's the only rule. A short intro shows it
on first launch, and you can read it again any time under **Settings → Help →
How to play**.

### How do I place a colour?
Tap an empty cell, then tap a colour from the tray at the bottom — or drag a
colour from the tray straight onto a cell.

### Can we play together?
Yes. Turn on **Two-player mode** in **Settings → Game rules**. Both players work
the same board as a team — it's about solving together, so there's no "winner".

### How do the hints work?
Tap the lightbulb. With **teaching mode** on, a hint is revealed step by step —
what to notice, why it holds, and the move it leads to — with the pattern drawn
on the board. Tap the banner to reveal the next step; tap anywhere else to skip
straight to the move. Turn teaching mode off in **Settings → Help** for quick
hints that just play the move with a short note.

### What do the difficulty levels mean?
**Easy**, **Medium**, **Hard**, and **Extreme**. Every level is designed so it
can be solved — and explained — with the techniques the hints actually teach, so
a hint is never a dead end. Extreme leans harder on the advanced patterns.

### Can I change how the board looks?
Yes. Use the view menu to switch between **gems, flat colours, numbers, or a
glow**, and choose a **board style**. Light and dark are both supported.

### What is the daily streak?
Solve at least one board a day to build your streak. Miss a day and it resets.
Your streak also shows as a flame at the top of the screen — tap it (or the best
time) to open your statistics. Turn on **Daily reminder** in **Settings → Game
rules** for a once-a-day nudge.

### Where do I see my statistics?
Tap the **flame** or the **best-time trophy** at the top of the game screen, or
open **Settings → Show statistics**. You'll find best and average times per
difficulty, accuracy, and your daily streak.

### What does "Allow mistakes" do?
With it off, wrong colours are rejected as you play. With it on, you can place a
wrong colour and fix it later. Either way, a board counts as solved only when
the whole grid is correct.

### Can I share a board or play one someone sent me?
Yes. You can save and share a board as a code, and import a code someone sends
you. Note: a hand-made board with more than one possible solution will still be
playable, but hints on it are less precise.

### Is BrainBro accessible?
BrainBro supports **VoiceOver**, **Dynamic Type**, and **Reduce Motion**.

### Which languages are supported?
Swedish, English, and German. BrainBro follows your device language, and you can
override it in the app's settings.

### What about privacy?
BrainBro keeps your games, statistics, and settings **on your device**. It
doesn't collect or transmit your personal data, and there are no ads. See the
[Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
