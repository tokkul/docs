# SecretBro Docs

Public documentation for **SecretBro**, a private password, pincode, and 2FA
manager for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Save your logins behind Face ID, generate strong passwords, keep two-factor
(TOTP) codes on your wrist, and let iOS fill your passwords for you with the
built-in AutoFill extension. Everything is encrypted on device and synced across
your own devices with iCloud — SecretBro has no accounts and no servers of its
own.
