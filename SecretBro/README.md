# SecretBro Docs

Public documentation for **SecretBro**, a private password, pincode, and 2FA
manager for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Save logins, cards, bank details, Wi-Fi, passports and notes behind Face ID —
each category shows the fields that fit it. Generate strong passwords, keep
two-factor (TOTP) codes on your wrist, import from CSV or 1Password (.1pif), and
let iOS fill your passwords for you with the built-in AutoFill extension.
Everything is encrypted on device and synced across your own devices with
iCloud — SecretBro has no accounts and no servers of its own.
