# Privacy Policy

**App:** EasyBro
**Last updated:** 2026-08-24

EasyBro is designed to be a simple, private phone launcher for people who want
big, easy buttons to call favourite contacts and open apps. Your privacy is
respected by design: **EasyBro does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any
personal data.**

## What data EasyBro uses

Everything you set up in EasyBro is stored **only on your device**:

- **Favourite contacts** — the name, phone number, and (optionally) photo you
  add for each call button.
- **App buttons and custom links** — the built-in apps you choose to show and
  any custom URL / Apple Shortcut links you add.
- **Appearance and settings** — colours, button sizes, language, time-of-day
  labels, and button order.

This information is saved on your device using Apple's standard local storage
(an App Group), so that the EasyBro app and its Home Screen widget can show the
same buttons. It is **never uploaded to us or to any third party.**

## Apple Watch

If you use the EasyBro Apple Watch app, you can choose one contact for its big
call button. Only that contact's **name and phone number** are sent from your
iPhone to your **own paired Apple Watch**, using Apple's standard on-device
connection between your phone and watch (WatchConnectivity). This stays between
your own devices — it is **never sent to us or to any third party** — and the
watch keeps it in local storage so the button works when your phone is nearby.

## Contacts permission

If you choose to import a contact from your address book, EasyBro asks for
permission to access your Contacts. This access is used **only** at the moment
you pick a contact, to copy that person's name, number, and photo into an
EasyBro call button. EasyBro does not read, store, or transmit the rest of your
address book, and you can add contacts manually without granting this
permission.

## What EasyBro does NOT do

- No accounts, sign-in, or registration.
- No analytics, usage tracking, advertising, or profiling.
- No third-party SDKs.
- No network connections — EasyBro makes no requests to any server.
- No location, microphone, camera, or health data access.

## Calls and opening other apps

When you tap a button, EasyBro asks iOS to place a call or open another app
(for example Messages, Maps, Weather, or a custom link). Those actions are
handled by iOS and the destination app under Apple's own privacy terms;
EasyBro is not involved once the call or app has started.

## Your control over your data

- Remove a contact or app button in EasyBro's settings to delete that
  information from your device.
- Deleting the EasyBro app removes all of its stored data from your device.

## Children

EasyBro does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
