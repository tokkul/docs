# Privacy Policy

**App:** LogBro
**Last updated:** 2026-08-17

LogBro is a note-taking app designed to keep your notes yours. Your privacy is
respected by design: **LogBro does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your
personal data with us or any third party.** LogBro has no accounts of its own,
no analytics, and no advertising. The only place your notes travel is your own
Apple iCloud account, and only if you keep iCloud enabled.

## What data LogBro uses

Everything you create in LogBro is your content:

- **Categories and notes** — the titles, text, bullet lists, checklists, and
  tags you write.
- **Attachments** — photos and videos you add to a note.
- **Category details** — cover images, notes info, and the order you arrange
  your categories in.
- **Appearance and settings** — colour scheme (light/dark), language, and
  similar preferences.

This information is stored **on your device** using Apple's standard local
storage (SwiftData in a shared App Group, so the LogBro app, its Home Screen
widget, and the Share extension can all read the same notes).

## iCloud sync and sharing

LogBro uses **Apple's iCloud (CloudKit)** to sync your notes across your own
devices and to let you share a category with other people.

- Your notes sync through **your own iCloud account**. LogBro does not run its
  own servers and never receives a copy of your notes. Data stored in iCloud is
  handled by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
  and iCloud terms.
- When you **share a category**, the people you invite can view (and, if you
  give them edit access) change the notes in that category. Sharing is handled
  by Apple's CloudKit Sharing. You choose who to invite, and you can stop
  sharing or remove a participant at any time.
- If you turn iCloud off (or sign out of iCloud), LogBro keeps working with the
  notes stored locally on that device; they simply won't sync.

## Photos and videos

When you add an image or video to a note, LogBro copies that file into its own
storage so it can display and sync it with the note. LogBro uses Apple's photo
picker, which lets you choose individual items without granting access to your
entire photo library.

## Importing and exporting

LogBro can export a category to a CSV file and import notes from a CSV file you
choose. These files are read from or written to the location **you** pick using
the system file picker. LogBro does not upload them anywhere.

## What LogBro does NOT do

- No LogBro accounts, sign-in, or registration (sync uses your own iCloud).
- No analytics, usage tracking, advertising, or profiling.
- No third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
- No developer-operated servers — LogBro does not send your notes to us.
- No location, microphone, contacts, or health data access.

## Your control over your data

- Edit or delete any note or category in the app to remove that content; the
  change syncs to your other devices and to anyone you've shared it with.
- Stop sharing a category, or remove a participant, at any time from the
  category's sharing options.
- Manage what LogBro keeps in iCloud from **Settings → [your name] → iCloud** on
  your device.
- Deleting the LogBro app removes its local data from that device. Content in
  iCloud is removed according to Apple's iCloud behaviour and your iCloud
  settings.

## Children

LogBro does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
