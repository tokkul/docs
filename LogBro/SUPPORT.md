# LogBro — Support

LogBro keeps your notes organised in categories, with a simple block editor for
text, bullet lists, checklists, and photos — all synced across your devices
with iCloud and easy to share with the people you choose.

## Contact

Questions, feedback, or trouble? Email **tokkul@gmail.com** and we'll help.
Please include your device model and iOS version.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I create a category and a note?
Categories are the top-level groups on the main screen. Tap **+** to add a
category, open it, then tap **+** (or start typing) to add a note inside it.

### How does the editor work?
Each note is made of blocks. Type to add paragraphs, and use the toolbar above
the keyboard to turn a line into a **bullet** or a **checkbox**, or to insert a
**photo**. Tap a checkbox to mark it done. Your changes save automatically.

### Can I dictate a note instead of typing?
Yes — using iOS's built-in dictation. Tap into a note block to bring up the
keyboard, then tap the **microphone key** (bottom row of the keyboard, next to
the space bar) and start speaking. Your words are typed straight into the block,
and you can switch back to the keyboard at any time. Speech is transcribed by
iOS, not by LogBro.

If you don't see the microphone key, enable it in **Settings → General →
Keyboard → Enable Dictation**. Dictation language follows your keyboard
language, and you can add more keyboards/languages in the same Keyboard
settings.

### How do I add photos or videos?
Tap the image button in the editor toolbar and pick from your photos. The item
is copied into the note and synced with it via iCloud.

### How do I tag and find notes?
Add a **tag** to a note to group related notes together. Use the search field
to find notes by title, text, or tag — searching from the main screen shows
matching notes across all your categories.

### Does LogBro sync across my devices?
Yes. LogBro syncs through **your own iCloud account**, so notes you create on
one device appear on your others. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud and that
iCloud is enabled for LogBro in **Settings → [your name] → iCloud**.

### How do I share a category with someone?
Swipe a category (or use its sharing option) and choose **Share**. Invite
people through the standard iOS sharing sheet and pick whether they can view or
edit. Invited people see the notes in that category and can collaborate. You can
stop sharing or remove someone at any time.

### How do I quickly capture a note from the Home Screen?
Add the **LogBro widget**: touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap
**+**, search for **LogBro**, and add it. Tapping it opens LogBro straight into
a new note.

### Can I send text, links, or images into LogBro from other apps?
Yes. Use the iOS **Share** sheet in another app (Safari, Photos, Notes, etc.)
and choose **LogBro** to save the text, URL, or images as a new note.

### Can I import or export my notes?
Yes. A category can be **exported to a CSV file**, and you can **import notes
from a CSV file** you choose using the system file picker.

### Which languages are supported?
English and Swedish. LogBro follows your device language and can be changed in
the app's settings.

### Something went wrong on launch / my notes didn't load. What can I do?
If LogBro reports that storage couldn't be opened, use the in-app **Reset Local
Cache and Resync from iCloud** option. It backs up and clears the local
database, then re-downloads your synced notes from iCloud on the next launch.
Note that local-only changes that were never synced may not be recoverable.

### What about privacy?
LogBro keeps your notes on your device and syncs them through **your own
iCloud** — it doesn't collect or transmit your data to us. See the
[Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
