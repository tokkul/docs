# Privacy Policy

**App:** ScanBro
**Last updated:** 2026-11-26

ScanBro is a box-logging app that keeps your scans on your device. **ScanBro
does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data with us or any
third party.** ScanBro has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising, and it
does not connect to the internet.

## What data ScanBro uses

- **Scans** — the value of each code you scan or type, its code type, whether
  it was incoming or outgoing, the time of the scan, and any note you add.
- **Settings** — the file name, delimiter, timestamp format, and header-row
  choice.

Scans are saved as a CSV file in ScanBro's own folder on your device, which you
can also see in the Files app under **On My iPhone → ScanBro**. Settings are
stored on your device using Apple's standard app preferences.

## Camera

ScanBro uses the camera only while the scan screen is open, to read QR codes and
barcodes. Codes are recognised on the device using Apple's VisionKit framework.
**Camera images are never saved, recorded, or sent anywhere**; only the text of
the code you scan is stored. You can turn camera access off at any time in
**Settings → ScanBro → Camera** and type codes instead.

## Importing and exporting

- **Export** — when you export, the CSV file goes to the destination **you**
  pick in the iOS share sheet (for example Mail, AirDrop, or Files). ScanBro
  doesn't send it anywhere else.
- **Import** — when you import a CSV file using the system file picker, ScanBro
  copies that file into its own folder. It can read only the file you choose.

## Backups

ScanBro's folder is included in your device's normal backups (iCloud Backup or a
backup to your computer), according to your own backup settings. Those backups
are handled by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## What ScanBro does NOT do

- No accounts, sign-in, or registration.
- No analytics, usage tracking, advertising, or profiling.
- No third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
- No servers and no network connections — your scans never leave your device
  unless you export them.
- No location, microphone, contacts, photo library, or health data access.

## Your control over your data

- Swipe left on a scan to delete it, or edit its note at any time.
- Delete a whole CSV file from **Settings** in the app, or from the Files app.
- Deleting the ScanBro app removes its files and settings from your device.

## Children

ScanBro does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
